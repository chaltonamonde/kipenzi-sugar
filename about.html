ABOUT KIPENZI SUGAR

Built on Farmer Trust & Industrial Excellence

Kipenzi Sugar Company Limited was founded with a singular purpose: to establish a modern, transparent, and farmer-centred agribusiness that unlocks the agricultural potential of Alego Usonga, Siaya County.

OUR MANDATE

Restoring Confidence in Cane

We operate under the legal frameworks of the Kenya Sugar Board, guaranteeing prompt weighbridge transparency, 7-day payouts, and agronomic partnership.

Our Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted, efficient, and community-integrated sugar milling enterprise, transforming Siaya County into a premier agribusiness growth pole.

Our Mission

To process 1,250 tonnes of high-quality sugarcane daily through state-of-the-art milling and 3 MW clean cogeneration, delivering guaranteed 7-day payments to outgrowers.

Our Core Values

Integrity: Zero-tolerance for weighbridge manipulation.
Punctuality: Farmer payment within 7 days.
Respect: Farmers as equal equity partners in our supply chain.

Addressing the Trust Gap

A New Paradigm for Western Kenya Sugar Farming

For decades, sugarcane farmers across the Nyando and Lake Victoria sugar belt have endured delayed cane harvesting, weighbridge cheating, unpredictable pricing, and months of waiting for their sweat to be paid.

Kipenzi Sugar Company Limited is intentionally built differently. Situated in Mur-Malanga, Alego Usonga, our 1,250 TCD factory is scaled precisely to our 15 km outgrower radius (10,000 hectares), ensuring that every mature cane stalk is harvested promptly between 18–22 months and crushed within 24 hours of cutting.

"Our strength lies in the soil of Alego Usonga and the hands of our outgrowers. If the farmer is paid on time, the factory runs continuously; when the factory runs continuously, the whole community thrives."

Factory Strategic Fundamentals

Location Advantage: Mur-Malanga Sub-Location, Bar Olengo, South East Alego, Alego Usonga, Siaya County. Excellent road connectivity to Siaya-Rang'ala corridor.
Factory Footprint: 1,250 TCD mill, 3 MW bagasse cogeneration, modern automated boiling house, digital weighbridge stations.
Regulatory Alignment: Compliance with Kenya Sugar Board provisions, National Environment Management Authority (NEMA) licensing, and Siaya County Government spatial plans.
