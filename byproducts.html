PRIMARY PRODUCT • 138 TPD

Kipenzi Refined Table Sugar

Direct-consumption fortified white and natural brown granulated table sugar. Extracted from freshly crushed Mur-Malanga cane without chemical bleaching agents.

  • • Packaging Options: 50 kg commercial sacks, 25 kg bags, and 1 kg / 2 kg retail packs.
  • • Target Markets: Supermarket retail chains, FMCG wholesalers, confectionery and bakeries.
  • • Monthly Volume: ~3,250 Metric Tonnes per month.

BY-PRODUCT • 40-50 TPD

High-Brix Industrial Molasses

Heavy, dense cane molasses with 78°–82° Brix and high fermentable sugars. Supplied in bulk tankers to commercial distillers and animal feed millers.

  • • Primary Applications: Potable alcohol distilling, bio-ethanol fuel blending, yeast production.
  • • Livestock Feed: Energy and mineral booster for dairy cattle and feedlot rations.
  • • Supply Method: Bulk tanker loading at Mur-Malanga factory gantry.

ENERGY • 3.0 MW COGENERATION

Bagasse Biomass & Clean Power

Fibrous residue from cane milling crushed in our rollers. High calorific value biomass utilized to power high-pressure steam boilers and generate 3 MW clean electricity.

  • • Surplus Biomass: Raw material for eco-friendly particle board, paper pulp, and briquettes.
  • • Zero Fossil Fuels: 100% renewable power generation with low carbon footprint.
  • • Industrial Steam: High efficiency thermal energy recycling throughout the plant.

BIO-FERTILISER • SOIL HEALTH

Organic Filter Mud & Boiler Ash

Precipitated cane impurities rich in organic phosphorus, calcium, nitrogen, and potassium. Composted into certified bio-organic soil conditioners.

  • • Soil Remediation: Neutralizes acidic soils and restores organic carbon in Siaya farms.
  • • Farmer Return Scheme: Distributed back to outgrowers at zero product cost to boost ratoon yields.
  • • Bulk Commercial Supply: Available for horticultural and commercial agricultural estates.
