Eco-Friendly Engineering

Environmental Safeguards in Mur-Malanga

State-of-the-art environmental engineering ensures that air, water, and soil systems around our factory site remain clean and protected.

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

Effluent treatment plant (ETP) treats factory wash waters through biological aeration and filtration. Clean water is recycled 100% within the mill cooling circuits.

Electrostatic Air Scrubbers

High-efficiency electrostatic precipitators capture 99%+ of particulate ash from boiler flue gases, guaranteeing clean air emissions exceeding NEMA standards.

Organic Bio-Nutrient Return

Filter mud and boiler ash are rich in phosphorus, nitrogen, and potassium. We compost and return 100% of these organic by-products back to farmers' soils.

Community Agreement

The Kipenzi Local Jobs & Social Charter

Our factory must directly change lives in Alego Usonga. We have enshrined specific commitments into our community partnership agreement:

✓ 80%+ Local Employment: Factory operators, logistics drivers, weighbridge clerks, and security personnel recruited locally in Alego Usonga.
✓ Feeder Road Grading: Regular grading, culvert installation, and all-weather murram resurfacing on cane access roads in Mur-Malanga and Bar Olengo.
✓ Clean Water Access: Equipping community boreholes and water kiosks near the factory site for public access.
✓ Education & Agronomy Bursary: Sponsoring local students for certificate and diploma training in sugar engineering and agriculture.
