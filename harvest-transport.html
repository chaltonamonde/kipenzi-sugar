Agronomic Timing

The Cane Maturity Guide: Why 18–22 Months Matters

Harvesting at the exact sucrose peak maximizes the farmer's tonnage payout and the factory's sugar extraction efficiency.

STAGE 1: 0 – 12 MONTHS

Vegetative & Tillering

Rapid vertical stalk extension and tillering. High moisture and fiber content, low sucrose concentration. Top-dressing fertilizer applied at 3–4 months.

Brix: < 10° • Not Harvestable
STAGE 2: 13 – 17 MONTHS

Sugar Accumulation

Growth slows as stalks ripen under tropical sunlight. Photosynthetic sugars convert to crystalline sucrose throughout the stalk internodes.

Brix: 14° – 17° • Maturing
STAGE 3: 18 – 22 MONTHS (PEAK)

Optimal Harvest Window

Trash naturally dries at the base; top internodes firm with concentrated sucrose. Field extension officers conduct refractometer brix field sampling.

Brix: 18° – 22° • HARVEST READY
