Zero Waste Agribusiness

Beyond Sugar: Complete Value Addition

Kipenzi utilizes every component of the sugarcane stalk, building renewable power, industrial alcohol, and bio-nutrients for sustainable farming.

3 MW Green Power

Bagasse biomass fuels high-pressure boilers, powering the mill with zero external fossil fuels.

Premium Molasses

High-sugar molasses supplied to regional distillers, ethanol plants, and livestock feed manufacturers.

Organic Filter Mud

Rich in nitrogen and phosphorus, returned directly to outgrower cane fields to enrich local soil.

Boiler Ash Conditioners

Potassium-rich ash recycled for eco-friendly building materials and soil pH conditioning.
