Legal Protection

The Cane Supply Contract Explained in Plain Language

We believe that trust begins with transparency. Every outgrower signs an official Cane Supply Agreement registered under the Kenya Sugar Board. Here are the core commitments in simple terms:

1. Miller's Obligation to Purchase

Kipenzi Sugar is legally obligated to harvest and accept all mature cane from contracted land between 18 and 22 months of crop age.

2. Seven-Day Payment Guarantee & Penalty Interest

Payments must be settled within seven calendar days of delivery. Under Sugar Board rules, any late payment incurs automatic statutory interest payable to the farmer.

3. Fair Weighbridge & Dispute Rights

Farmers or their designated cooperative representatives have the legal right to witness weighbridge measurements and request independent re-calibration.

4. Input Credit Transparency

Seed, fertilizer, and tractor services are supplied at pre-agreed costs with zero hidden administrative markups, documented on your farmer ledger.

Farmer Rights & Miller Duties

Farmer Entitlements:
  • Timely harvest when cane reaches 18–22 months.
  • Digital receipt and SMS voucher immediately upon weighing.
  • Full payment via M-Pesa or bank transfer within 7 days.
  • Dispute escalation to the Sugar Directorate Tribunal.
Farmer Responsibilities:
  • Adhere to agronomy guidelines (spacing, weeding, fertilizer application).
  • Deliver cane exclusively to Kipenzi Sugar under the contract (prohibiting cane poaching).
  • Permit field officers access for crop health inspections.
