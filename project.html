PROCESSING WORKFLOW

From Mur-Malanga Weighbridge to Packaged Sugar

Automated SCADA Control System
Stage 1
Weighbridge & Tare

Automated digital weighbridges compute gross and tare weight; core brix sampler takes sucrose reading.

Stage 2
Milling & Extraction

High-efficiency multi-roller mill extracts cane juice. Bagasse is diverted to the 3 MW boiler furnaces.

Stage 3
Clarification & Boiling

Juice is clarified, filtered, and concentrated in vacuum pans. Filter mud is returned to farmers as fertilizer.

Stage 4
Crystallisation & Sugar

Centrifugals separate sugar crystals from molasses. Sugar is dried, graded, and packaged for commercial distribution.

RENEWABLE ENERGY

3 MW Cogeneration Plant

Kipenzi uses bagasse (the fibrous residue of crushed sugarcane) to generate high-pressure steam and drive steam turbo-alternators. The plant generates 3.0 MW of clean electricity, meeting 100% of internal factory parasitic power requirements with zero diesel or coal reliance.

  • • Reduces environmental footprint and greenhouse gas emissions.
  • • High-pressure multi-fuel boiler design with modern electrostatic precipitators.
  • • Excess power potential for future micro-grid export or local cold storage facilities.
VALUE ADDITION

Distillery & Molasses Utilization

Sugar milling generates heavy industrial molasses. Kipenzi's integrated distillery plan converts this by-product into valuable industrial products, creating diversified revenue streams and shielding the business from commodity sugar price fluctuations.

  • • High-purity extra neutral alcohol (ENA) for commercial and pharmaceutical uses.
  • • Liquid livestock feed supplements for dairy farmers in Siaya County.
  • • Zero effluent discharge with advanced vinasse concentration and composting.
