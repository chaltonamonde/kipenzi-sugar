High Yield Guidelines

The 5 Pillars of Sugarcane Productivity

Follow our practical, field-tested recommendations to achieve 80–120 tonnes of cane per hectare in Alego Usonga.

1. Deep Land Preparation

Plow to a minimum depth of 30–35 cm to break soil hardpans. Harrow thoroughly and construct furrows spaced 1.4 to 1.5 meters apart to promote deep root aeration.

2. Certified Seed Cane (Setts)

Use only 3-bud setts sourced from Kipenzi certified disease-free nurseries. Treat against smut and ratoon stunting disease before planting in continuous overlapping rows.

3. Balanced Soil Nutrition

Apply DAP or NPK basal fertilizer in the furrow at planting (2 bags/Ha). Top-dress with CAN or urea at 3 to 4 months during the start of the rains, paired with organic filter mud.

4. Weeding & Earthing-Up

Keep fields 100% weed-free during the first 4 months before canopy closure. Earth-up cane rows at 4–5 months to anchor roots against wind lodging.

5. Ratoon Crop Care

Immediately after harvest, shave stubbles level with the soil, apply trash blanketing between rows to retain moisture, and apply ratoon booster fertilizer within 3 weeks.
