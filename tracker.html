REGULATORY READINESS

Aligning Crop Maturity with Factory Commissioning

Sugarcane requires 18 to 22 months to reach peak sucrose maturity in Alego Usonga's climate. Kipenzi is synchronizing seed cane distribution so that first harvest schedules directly coincide with final mill dry-run testing and the issuance of the commercial milling license from the Sugar Directorate / Kenya Sugar Board.
