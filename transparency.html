How Weighbridge & Sucrose Testing Works

Every kilogram of cane and unit of sucrose is verified through automated, tamper-proof electronic instruments with farmer inspection rights.

Step 1

Gross Vehicle Weighing

Loaded tractor or truck enters calibrated 60-tonne electronic weighbridge. Gross weight logged into the server automatically.

Step 2

Unloading & Tare Weighing

After cane is unloaded into the feeder table, the empty vehicle is re-weighed. Net Cane = Gross Weight − Tare Weight.

Step 3

Core Sucrose / Brix Sampling

Automated core sampler extracts random stalks. Laboratory digital refractometers determine pol/brix purity levels.

Step 4

Instant SMS & Portal Receipt

Within 5 minutes of weighing, an automated SMS reaches the farmer's registered phone with Net Tonnes, Slip ID, and Payout Date.

Farmer Protection

How to Raise a Weighbridge or Payment Dispute

Under the Kipenzi Cane Supply Contract, every farmer has an open, legally binding pathway to contest weighbridge tickets or deduction amounts without fear of retribution.

Stage 1 (Immediate): Request on-the-spot re-weighing with the Chief Weighbridge Supervisor before cane is unloaded into the hopper.
Stage 2 (48 Hours): Lodge a written grievance at the Mur-Malanga Liaison Desk. Extension Manager reviews the core laboratory brix sample.
Stage 3 (Statutory Escalation): If unresolved, refer the matter to the independent Kenya Sugar Board Dispute Resolution Committee.
